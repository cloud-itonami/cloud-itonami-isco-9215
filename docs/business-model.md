# Business Model: Forestry Site Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9215`
- ISCO-08: `9215`
- Occupation: Forestry Labourers
- Social impact: worker-safety, terrain-hazard-mitigation, production-continuity

## Customer

- forestry operations / timber estates / conservation and land-management crews
- independent forestry labour crews / forestry-labour cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- task/progress-record logging
- forestry-supplies procurement coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to site safety supervisors

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a forestry-work-execution decision (e.g.
  authorizing a felling or clearing operation to proceed), ever
- no direct finalization of a site-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a site cleared for safety), ever
- no override of a site safety supervisor's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (falling-tree/branch hazard, uneven-terrain
  hazard, equipment-condition hazard, weather exposure) always route to
  human sign-off, regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
